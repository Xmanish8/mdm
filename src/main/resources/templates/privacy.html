Information used to run the workspace

MDM stores member names, email addresses, password hashes, roles, team memberships, work items, comments and activity history. Authorized team members and administrators can access information according to their permissions. Do not put passwords, access keys or unnecessary sensitive personal information in work items.

Security and essential storage

The service uses an essential session cookie to maintain sign-in and protect form submissions. It expires after inactivity or sign-out. Your browser stores your cookie preference for up to 180 days. Security throttling stores keyed hashes of account/IP combinations and request counters, not raw addresses in that counter table. Expired counters are periodically removed after one day. Infrastructure providers may separately retain operational logs under the operator's policy.

Optional analytics

If you choose analytics, MDM counts views of this introduction, privacy and terms pages by page and date. It sends no user ID, email, IP address, query string, work-item content or advertising identifier to the analytics table. It does not load an external analytics service. Aggregate rows older than 30 days are periodically removed.

Optional analytics is disabled for this deployment. No analytics request is sent.

You can choose essential storage only and still use every workspace feature. Cookie preferences can be changed through the footer. Withdrawal stops future optional page counting; aggregate counts already recorded cannot be attributed back to you.

Hosting, retention and requests

The operator selects the hosting region, infrastructure providers, backup policy and retention period for organizational work and audit records. These records are retained until the operator applies its documented retention process; automatic work-item deletion is not implemented. Administrators should keep access limited to current authorized members. Contact the operator to request access, corrections, removal or information about your organization's retention practices.
